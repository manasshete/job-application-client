## DOCUMENT GENERATION EXPLAINED

### What counts as a "document"?
- **1 Tailored Resume** = 1 document
- **1 Cover Letter** = 1 document
- **1 Resume + 1 Cover Letter pair** = 2 documents

### Example Usage:
**Pro Tier (200 documents/month)**
- Apply to 100 jobs with tailored resumes = 100 documents
- Generate 100 matching cover letters = 100 documents
- **Total: 200 documents used ✅**

**Premium Tier (500 documents/month)**
- Apply to 250 jobs with tailored resumes = 250 documents
- Generate 250 matching cover letters = 250 documents
- **Total: 500 documents used ✅**

### Monthly Reset:
- Usage resets on the 1st of each month
- Unused documents do NOT carry over
- Usage counter visible in dashboard

## FAQ

**Q: Can I switch tiers?**
A: Yes, anytime. Changes take effect immediately. Pro-rata billing applied.

**Q: What happens if I exceed my document quota?**
A: Generation is blocked until next month or upgrade to higher tier.

**Q: Do unused documents roll over?**
A: No, monthly quotas reset on the 1st. Plan accordingly.

**Q: Can I share my account with a team?**
A: Create separate accounts (each person gets own tier) or consider Premium for agency use.

**Q: What if I need more documents mid-month?**
A: Upgrade to Premium for 500/month or wait for monthly reset.

**Q: How does job extraction work?**
A: Paste a job URL → AI extracts company, position, salary, location, description automatically.

**Q: Can I use old resumes with AI tailoring?**
A: Yes! Upload your existing resume, AI customizes it for each job.

**Q: Are generated documents saved?**
A: Generated content is shown in-app. You can download as PDF or save manually.
