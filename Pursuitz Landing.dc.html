FAQ

Questions worth asking

How does the link extraction actually work?+

Paste a job URL and Pursuitz reads the posting, pulls company, role, location, salary and requirements, and fills a new card on your board. It works with LinkedIn, Indeed, ATS portals and company career pages — and you can always create or edit a card by hand.

Will the AI invent experience I do not have?+

No. Tailoring re-weights and rewrites the experience already in your master CV so the relevant parts lead and the language matches the description. Nothing is fabricated. AI output can still be imperfect, so review before you submit.

Are the exports really ATS-friendly?+

Yes — clean single-column layouts with no tables, graphics or unusual fonts, exported as PDF or plain text. Those are the formats applicant tracking systems parse most reliably.

What can I do on the free plan?+

Track up to 10 applications, run 5 extractions a month, and generate 2 tailored resumes and cover letters a month. Enough to run the full workflow end to end before you decide to pay anything.

How do tokens and roll-over work?+

Extraction and document tokens are valid for 60 days from the day they land in your account, whether from a purchase, an upgrade or a downgrade. Unused tokens roll over on top of new ones and are always spent oldest-first.

Is my resume data safe?+

Everything is encrypted at rest and in transit, with row-level security isolating your account from every other. We never share or sell your information.
